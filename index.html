Hues & Cues

Players

In seating order (the cue giver passes the iPad clockwise). Tap a colour to change it.

    Each player is the cue giver

      How to play

      1. The cue giver looks at a card of 4 colours in private and chooses one.
      2. They give a one-word cue. It can't be a colour name or point to letters, numbers or places on the board.
      3. Going clockwise, every other player taps a square for their first cone. Squares can't be shared.
      4. The cue giver gives a second cue of up to two words. Going anticlockwise, everyone places a second cone.
      5. Reveal: 3 points on the colour, 2 inside the frame around it, 1 just outside the frame. The cue giver gets 1 point for every cone inside the frame.
      6. The game ends when everyone has been cue giver (twice each with 3–6 players, once with 7+). Most points wins.

      These board colours are an approximation of the printed board.
